# TATAFA / Private Island Masterplan

Design read: An architectural investor presentation for a private island; the real shoreline and the fixed project composition are more important than decorative motion.
Concept spine: The island as a navigable survey instrument. Every visual section points back to one registered masterplan, so a viewer always understands where the object belongs.
Delivery tier: cinema. One restrained generated aerial film provides the scroll journey; exact spatial exploration uses fixed image assets rather than generated motion geometry.
Animation mode: animated-website
Journey shape: single-shot. One continuous top-down 12-15 second aerial drift over the already assembled island. No scene cuts or invented morphing structures.
Journey: (1) complete island, TATAFA, whole island visible; (2) arrival/marina, a subtle move toward western access; (3) inhabited coast, private villas; (4) eastern amenities and overwater routes; (5) final hold on the complete island. Semantic HTML labels remain available outside the film.
World grammar: Aerial 90-degree nadir, north neutral, still water with visible reef, ocean blue and botanical green, pale limestone, daylight with the light from upper left. The scroll film is explicitly marked concept visualization, while the exact map is the source for positions.
Mobile framing: letterbox/center-safe aerial subject, responsive map with accessible list alternative, lighter 720p video, reduced-motion static gallery.
Delivery budget: all desktop film <=32 MiB; all mobile film <=16 MiB.

Locked palette: #071b23 deep ocean ink, #e5ece8 mineral white, #78b5ad muted sea-glass accent, #9aaeb0 mist-grey, #183d40 vegetation shadow. This cool saltwater palette is taken from the reef, avoiding neon or beige-and-brass luxury clichés.
Locked type: Outfit display with IBM Plex Mono micro-data. Crisp architectural sans works with photographic rather than heritage luxury.
Design dials: variance 7, motion 6, density 2. Page theme is cool dark throughout. Corners nearly square (2px); image shapes follow source geometry.
Combinatorial pick: Deep Dark / full-bleed cinematic imagery / Swiss rational sans with hard hierarchy / massive image-first with restrained text / gallery-led cadence / layered image crop frames + hover-accordion slices + off-grid editorial + oversized fact strip / journey-waypoints / one macro crop of glass domes as second-read moment.
No prior website-build identity in this conversation to repeat.

Section plan (each has distinct dominant family):
1. Full-screen final-vision island still under the scroll film, with a minimal lower-left title.
2. EXISTING: a bright documentary split image with a small factual sidebar and unverified dimensions explicitly withheld.
3. Transition: a pinned film journey with semantic chapters and state labels, no claim of exact geometric morph.
4. PROPOSED: edge-to-edge clickable masterplan with image-coordinate hotspots and synchronized zoomed object view.
5. Project atlas: asymmetrical horizontal object-selector strip and a single large visual stage, showing supplied site-specific references.
6. Infrastructure: sparse route diagram over the same island image, only confirmed paths drawn; energy/water/waste/comms and runway marked location pending.
7. Development: one repeated island view with three selectable conceptual phases, no invented dates or costs.
8. Final: full-bleed island photograph and an unobtrusive factual disclaimer.

Asset plan: use the client's approved island montage and prior generated approved site-specific marina, villas, dome district, civic center, manta and retail concepts as immutable visual sources. Generate one six-frame film storyboard, one reference board per section, one continuity-focused aerial film, bespoke launch branding (cover/OG/favicon) and a small icon suite only for facts; hero image is the approved masterplan. The film must never replace exact map evidence.
CTA inventory: Hero 'Открыть карту' is a viewport-target bracket; map hotspots are spatial pins with visible focus ring; atlas rows are image-reveal selectors; final back-to-top is a discrete right-aligned text route. One control design per intent.
Data discipline: EXISTING = source satellite only; PROPOSED = user-composited masterplan; CONCEPT VISUALIZATION = AI renders. No area, coastline length, construction dates, financing, or runway siting without owner data. Previous 6,141.65 sqm measurement concerned a selected shopping-center plot, not total island area. Conceptual aircraft and villa counts are not technical specifications unless the owner verifies them.
